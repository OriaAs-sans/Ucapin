# Competitor English-Speaking-Practice Products: How Their Speaking Features Work

Research date: 2026-11-16. Source quality warning up front: a large share of the web content on this category is written by competing apps (SpeakShark, EngVarta, TalkDrill, Practice Me, LanguaTalk/Langua, Unstar) and is affiliate- or SEO-driven. LanguaTalk reviews are the most detailed hands-on teardowns found, but LanguaTalk promotes its own app (Langua). Pricing numbers from third parties conflict heavily; only figures read from official pages are treated as reliable.

## 1. How do the leading products structure speaking practice (core loop, screens/steps)?

### Takeaway
The market splits into three archetypes: (a) scripted "repeat-after-me" pronunciation drills with scoring (ELSA, Speak's Speaking Drills); (b) goal-bound AI roleplay with a few tasks to complete (Speak Roleplay, ELSA AI, Praktika topics, Gliglish); (c) open "phone/video call" with an AI character with little or no correction in-call (Duolingo Video Call, Speak Free Talk, Loora, Talkpal call mode). Strong products mix (a) and (b) in a structured path; the gap most reviewers point to is weak linkage between conversation errors and subsequent practice.

### Cited Findings
**Speak (usespeak / speak.com)**
- Course has five lesson types: Tutor Lesson (guided, sometimes video, introduces pronunciation/vocab; common early, rare later); Speaking Drill (sentence repetition, words light up as you say them); Vocab Builder (matching, fill-in-the-blank); Roleplay (scenario using the unit's vocabulary); Tutor Q&A (AI asks a question expecting a specific answer). Roleplay appears from upper-elementary level. Review updated 2 Nov 2026, rated 3/5 — [LanguaTalk Speak review](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- Roleplay: pick a scenario, complete three tasks; ends when tasks are done or conversation naturally concludes — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- Free Talk: user sets roles, topic and setting; AI handles nuance well but tends to end every reply with a question (felt forced) — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- Speak Tutor: an AI assistant you can ask grammar questions or ask to generate custom lessons/scenarios on the spot; grammar explanations are short and text-only — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/); [Speak official site](https://www.speak.com/)
- Smart Review uses spaced repetition to resurface course phrases with a mastery score, but in drill form; saved phrases cannot be reused in conversations — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- "Made for You" (Premium Plus only) generates drill lessons from your mistakes, unlocks only after a Free Talk session — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- Hands-on piece frames Speak's design philosophy as building "micro-fluency" rather than memorising words — [The Neuron](https://www.theneuron.ai/explainer-articles/hands-on-with-speaks-ai-language-tutor-stop-memorizing-words-and-start-building-micro-fluency/)

**ELSA Speak**
- Official product page lists: 8,000+ lessons, personalized learning path "based on your goals and level", AI role-play ("real-life role-plays and conversations"), advanced AI coach, bilingual AI tutor, guided practice (interviews, presentations, exams), transcripts and vocabulary suggestions, certificate courses (IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC), word-stress games — [ELSA product page](https://elsanow.io/en/product)
- Speech Analyzer: listens to natural, unscripted speech and gives a breakdown of communication skills; also runs in the browser alongside Zoom/Google Meet, filtering other voices out of the transcript so only the user is scored; ELSA advises a quiet space for accuracy — [ELSA help: Speech Analyzer](https://elsanow.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/31000178887-coach-speech-analyzer); [ELSA FAQ Speech Analyzer web](https://elsaspeak.com/en/faqs/speech-analyzer-web-version-2)
- A review characterises ELSA as primarily "drill software" rather than "conversation software", with limited cross-session memory in AI role-plays — (via search summary of third-party reviews; see [SoftwareFinder ELSA](https://softwarefinder.com/lms/elsa-speak))

**Praktika**
- Onboarding = learning-path setup: daily study time, goal, skills, CEFR level (A1–C1), interests from a preset list. Can't mix skills across goals; changing goal resets progress. Lesson titles vague, no content preview; can skip ahead. Review updated 23 June 2026, rated 3/5 — [LanguaTalk Praktika review](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)
- Animated avatar tutor sits at the top of every chat (7 avatars in French/Spanish); can be minimised but not turned off; no audio-only mode; lip-sync imperfect, expressions "uncanny" — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)
- Practice tab has ~140 conversation topics; outside these only Free Talk or a constrained custom scenario; AI steers drifting conversations back to the lesson objective — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)

**Duolingo Video Call (Lily)** — official blog, 24 Sept 2024 (older info)
- Entry points: a video-camera icon on certain path nodes, plus Practice Hub. Pre-call card "Video call: Lily" with Call / Skip. Lily picks up and opens the conversation; screen shows "Calling Lily…" and a red hang-up button — [Duolingo blog: Video Call](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call)
- Calls last ~1 minute early in a course, up to ~3 minutes later; unlimited repeats; can skip the node when in a noisy place and come back — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call)
- Lily adapts to level and comfort; personality (sarcasm) increases as course advances — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call)
- Max-only at launch; English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (iOS also Japanese/Korean) — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call); some non-Max users reportedly got access — [Duoplanet newsletter](https://newsletter.duoplanet.com/posts/duolingo-video-call-lily)
- Roleplay is the text-based sibling feature in Duolingo Max; Video Call differs by being spoken — [Duolingo Max blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-max); [Duolingo AI and Video Call blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/ai-and-video-call)

**Loora**
- Voice-based AI English speaking partner marketed on pronunciation, grammar fixes, rephrasing and accent; lesson summaries and personalized coaching tips — [Loora official site](https://loora.com); [Shyft listing](https://www.shyft.ai/tools/loora)
- Single tutor voice; no option to switch accents, personalities or teaching styles (competitor's claim) — [Practice Me vs Loora](https://practiceme.app/vs/loora)

**Talkpal / Gliglish**
- Talkpal modes include roleplay, debate and call modes, plus pronunciation assessment and progress tracking — [Tooldirectory Talkpal](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/talkpal); [LanguaTalk TalkPal review](https://languatalk.com/blog/talkpal-review/)
- Gliglish is a browser-first "speak with AI" tool with roleplays of real-life situations — [Gliglish official](https://gliglish.com/); [Gliglish Help](https://gliglish.com/help)

**Cambly**
- Human-tutor marketplace (live video calls with native speakers); no free tier; price hidden until country is known — [SpeakShark Cambly alternatives (competitor)](https://speakshark.com/blog/cambly-alternatives). I found no reliable source describing a Cambly AI tutor product.

### Inferences
- A typical AI-roleplay exercise across products has these screens: (1) scenario/topic picker (card with title, your role, AI role, goal/tasks); (2) optional prep (key phrases/vocab); (3) conversation screen with avatar or audio waveform, push-to-talk or auto-detect mic, running transcript, help buttons (hint/translate/repeat/slow down); (4) end trigger (tasks completed or natural end); (5) results screen with scores and corrections; (6) optional "practice your mistakes" drill.
- Duolingo's design (short 1–3 min calls, no in-call correction, AI opens the call, "skip if noisy") is a deliberate low-anxiety pattern well suited to Indonesian learners who report being shy/afraid of mistakes, but it gives little learning signal on its own.
- Web-first is underserved: most leaders are mobile apps; Gliglish and ELSA's Speech Analyzer web are the notable browser offerings.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch Speak's own help center for exact screen-by-screen flows, or Talkpal/Gliglish hands-on teardowns.
- Duolingo's newest (2025–2026) changes to Video Call (e.g., transcripts, recap, broader access) not verified; blog post is from 2024.
- No reliable info on Cambly AI features as of late 2026.

## 2. What feedback do they give?

### Takeaway
Feedback ranges from deep pronunciation scoring (ELSA) to light, lenient conversation feedback (Speak, Praktika). The most common reviewer criticism is that conversational feedback is brief, lenient, and not fed back into later practice. ELSA AI's four-part post-roleplay report (Overall, Pronunciation, Vocabulary, Grammar + tips) is the clearest documented results-screen template.

### Cited Findings
- ELSA AI (roleplay): after completing a conversation the result has four parts — Overall feedback, Pronunciation, Vocabulary, Grammar — plus tips to improve — [ELSA FAQ: ELSA AI feature](https://elsaspeak.com/faqs/elsa-ai-feature-2)
- ELSA official page: real-time corrections cover "fluency, intonation, pronunciation, grammar, and vocabulary"; page does not mention phoneme-level scoring or colour coding explicitly — [ELSA product page](https://elsanow.io/en/product)
- ELSA Speech Analyzer evaluates intonation, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation and fluency, with separate scoring weights for AI Roleplays vs Speech Analyzer — [ELSA help](https://elsanow.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/31000178887-coach-speech-analyzer)
- ELSA described as giving targeted feedback on individual words, stress, intonation, with replay and highlighted trouble spots; a competitor calls its scoring the deepest phoneme-level in the category — [SpeakShark Speak vs ELSA (competitor)](https://speakshark.com/blog/speak-vs-elsa-speak)
- Speak: words light up as you say them in drills; after roleplay a short error summary with option to generate a review lesson of Speaking Drills; no discussion of grammar patterns; recognition "overly lenient" — mispronunciations and reversed word order can still get a perfect score; Standard Premium has no cumulative mistake tracking — [LanguaTalk Speak review](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- Praktika: every spoken line has a "Feedback" button revealing corrections/suggestions (appears even with no mistake); no end-of-lesson summary, categorized error list or follow-up exercises; errors don't influence future lessons; recognition forgiving, misses subtle grammar/word-order errors — [LanguaTalk Praktika review](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)
- Praktika's pronunciation feedback is described as broader and less actionable than ELSA's — (search summary of third-party reviews; [Practice Me vs Praktika (competitor)](https://practiceme.app/vs/praktika))
- Duolingo Video Call: Lily does not correct grammar during the call, no penalty for mistakes; the 2024 post does not describe transcripts or a post-call recap — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call)
- Loora: real-time feedback on pronunciation, grammar and rephrasing; one review says phonetic correction targets individual sounds, not just words; lesson summaries — [Loora](https://loora.com); [Aichief Loora](https://aichief.com/ai-tools/loora/)
- Gliglish: limited feedback cited as a drawback — [Aichief Gliglish](https://aichief.com/ai-education-tools/giglish/)

### Inferences
- A differentiator for a new platform: combine ELSA-grade phoneme feedback with conversation, i.e., score pronunciation inside roleplay turns (not only in scripted drills), show a categorized recap (pronunciation / grammar / vocabulary / fluency), offer "better phrasing" suggestions, and auto-generate the next drill from that session's errors (the loop Speak locks behind Premium Plus and Praktika lacks entirely).
- Leniency is a real tension: lenient ASR keeps beginners motivated (praised as "accurate recognition") but advanced learners call it useless. A "strictness" setting or level-dependent thresholds could address both.
- Background knowledge (not verified in this session, treat as unconfirmed): ELSA historically colour-codes words/sounds green/yellow/red and shows which phoneme was mispronounced with mouth/tongue guidance.

### Gaps
- No primary source fetched confirming ELSA's current colour-coding/phoneme UI in 2026.
- No published data on accuracy of any app's ASR for Indonesian-accented English.

## 3. How do AI conversation partners / roleplay scenarios work (picking, hints, translation, turn-taking, recap)?

### Takeaway
Scenario picking is via curated libraries (Praktika ~140 topics; Speak unit-bound roleplays; ELSA everyday scenarios) plus a "custom/free talk" option. Help tools are typically: tap-word definitions, ask-to-repeat/slow-down, native-language scaffolding at beginner levels. Recaps are the weakest and most inconsistent element.

### Cited Findings
- Speak Roleplay: three tasks per scenario; scenarios use unit vocabulary; you can ask Speak Tutor for a custom scenario; scenarios reportedly don't get more complex at higher levels — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/); [search summary of reviews incl. Headway](https://makeheadway.com/blog/speak-app-review/)
- Praktika: beginner chats lean heavily on English (native-language scaffolding), intermediate fully in target language; app often asks learners to guess meaning from context (sometimes too little info); tap any word for definition and add to personal dictionary; no spaced repetition of saved words; no post-lesson recap, corrections visible only in transcript — [LanguaTalk Praktika](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)
- Duolingo Video Call: AI initiates; learner can say "What? I don't understand" or ask Lily to repeat/slow down; Lily pauses "thinking" to make turn-taking feel natural — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call)
- ELSA: "bilingual AI tutor" and "transcripts and vocabulary suggestions" listed as features (limited on Free) — [ELSA product page](https://elsanow.io/en/product)
- Gliglish free conversations capped at 50 messages — [Gliglish Help](https://gliglish.com/help)
- Category-wide, "AI conversation loops and stays shallow" accounts for 22% of 1–3 star reviews across Speak, ELSA, TalkPal, Loora, Learna (Unstar analysis, method not independently verified) — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026)

### Inferences
- For Indonesian learners, Bahasa Indonesia scaffolding (hint in Indonesian, "say it in Indonesian and I'll show the English", tap-to-translate) at A1–A2, fading out by B1, mirrors Praktika's approach and addresses fear of getting stuck.
- To avoid "shallow loops," roleplays need explicit goals/tasks (Speak's 3-task pattern), escalating difficulty, and memory of the learner across sessions (a gap noted for ELSA).
- Locally relevant scenarios (job interview at a BUMN/startup, ojol/driver, customer service/BPO, hospitality in Bali, IELTS speaking Part 1–3, campus presentation) would be a content differentiator; no competitor was found localizing scenarios for Indonesia.

### Gaps
- No detailed data on how Talkpal or Loora structure hints/recaps.
- No usage data on which help features learners actually use.

## 4. What do users praise and complain about? Indonesia-specific players and pain points

### Takeaway
Praise: natural voices, low-pressure practice, polished UI, good beginner content. Complaints: billing/trial traps (top complaint), shallow/repetitive AI, paywalled depth, lenient or wrong speech recognition (including penalising accented but clear speech). Indonesia's incumbent Cakap is human-teacher-focused; ELSA has an Indonesian presence; an AI player (SpeakShark) markets Indonesian-L1-tuned scoring. Indonesian learners' key phonological problems are well documented: /v/→/f/, /θ/→/t/, /ð/→/d/, /ʃ/→/s/, and final-consonant omission/devoicing.

### Cited Findings
**Complaints / praise**
- Category complaint shares in 1–3 star reviews (Speak, ELSA, TalkPal, Loora, Learna combined): weekly subscription auto-charged/hard to cancel 26%; conversation loops/shallow 22%; depth paywalled 18%; progress slower than promised 16%; speech recognition misjudges user 12%. iOS ratings: Speak 4.8, ELSA 4.8, TalkPal 4.7, Loora 4.9, Learna 4.6 — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026)
- ELSA: most lessons paywalled, free tier too thin; recognition sometimes penalises clear accented speech — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026); refund/cancellation difficulty — [Trustpilot ELSA](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/elsaspeak.com); usability complaints (onboarding, dated/confusing UI, mic bugs) — [Product Hunt ELSA](https://www.producthunt.com/tech/elsa)
- Speak: praised for natural voices, accurate recognition, engaging roleplay, polished UI, beginner pacing; criticised for brief feedback, limited variety at higher levels, drill-only review, few accents, confusing premium tiers; app-store reviews say recognition is too lenient — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/); premium pricing and repetitiveness after first sessions — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026)
- Praktika: billing/deceptive-trial complaints; faulty voice recognition; "very basic"; praised as low-pressure and human-like — [Trustpilot Praktika](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/praktika.ai/location/headquarter); [AI Tools Atlas](https://aitoolsatlas.ai/tools/praktika/worth-it); avatars distracting, no audio-only mode, rigid paths — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)
- Loora: most expensive in group; weekly billing and slow progress complaints — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026); dissatisfaction with pricing, billing management and speech recognition — [Sikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/en/loora-us)
- TalkPal: broad language coverage, low friction; loops and shallow conversations, paywall complaints — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/is-speak-elsa-talkpal-loora-learna-ai-english-apps-legit-2026)
- Reddit: no direct Reddit threads were retrieved; a vendor-adjacent blog claims Speak is the most-recommended AI conversation app on Reddit in 2026 — [EngVarta](https://engvarta.com/tag/english-speaking-apps-reddit/) (treat cautiously)

**Indonesia-specific players**
- Cakap: mainly human-teacher online courses; English adult courses from IDR 67,800 per session; free Cakap Club and Cakap Chat; curriculum with Sanata Dharma; no evidence found of an AI speaking coach — [Cakap package price](https://cakap.com/en/cakap-package-price/); [Cakap App Store](https://apps.apple.com/id/app/cakap/id1434645453)
- ELSA expanded to Indonesia, subscriptions reported from ~Rp 84k/month (older article, likely outdated) — [DailySocial](https://en.dailysocial.id/post/elsa-application-to-learn-to-speak-English)
- SpeakShark markets itself in Indonesia as AI conversation with phoneme scoring tuned for Indonesian L1 errors, full Indonesian UI, real free tier (self-published claim) — [SpeakShark Indonesia guide](https://speakshark.com/best-english-speaking-app-in-indonesia)

**Indonesian learner pronunciation pain points**
- Typical substitutions: /v/→/f/, /θ/→/t/, /ð/→/d/, /ʃ/→/s/, /ʒ/→/s/ (Aceh/Gayo learners) — [CORE: Errors in pronunciation of consonants by Indonesian, Gayo and Acehnese learners](https://core.ac.uk/works/24246925)
- Dental fricatives /θ ð/ and /v/ are absent from learners' native inventory — [IAIN Palopo IDEAS journal](https://ejournal.iainpalopo.ac.id/index.php/ideas/article/download/7500/6762/45977)
- Omission errors (27%) common in word-final consonants; errors concentrated in final stops [p b t d k g] and final sibilants [s z]; word-final voicing contrasts (e.g., /p/ vs /b/) cause breakdowns — [UNP Linguadidaktika PRAAT study](https://ejournal.unp.ac.id/index.php/linguadidaktika/article/view/133711); [NINJAL Cahyono paper](https://www2.ninjal.ac.jp/past-projects/phonology/Cahyono.pdf); [MDPI Languages](https://mdpi.com/2226-471X/9/6/222)
- Walisongo University thesis: /v/ most frequent consonant problem (26.64%), then /θ/ (13.19%) — (search summary of local thesis; small sample, indicative only)

### Inferences
- A platform for Indonesians should ship targeted minimal-pair modules: very/fery, think/tink, they/day, she/see, bed/bet, cab/cap, plus final-consonant and plural/past-tense endings (-s, -ed), since these map directly to grammar errors too.
- ASR must be tuned/evaluated on Indonesian-accented speech; "penalising clear accented speech" is a known category complaint and would be fatal for trust.
- Transparent pricing (monthly option, clear trial reminders, no weekly-subscription traps) is a cheap differentiator given billing is the #1 complaint.

### Gaps
- No Ruangguru English Academy / other Indonesian AI-speaking product details found within this research budget.
- No Indonesian-specific app-store review analysis found.
- No study found on Indonesian learners' stress/intonation problems (only segmental).

## 5. Pricing models and freemium limits

### Takeaway
Freemium with daily-minute caps (Gliglish, Talkpal ~10 min/day) or feature-limited free tiers (ELSA) dominate; premium AI conversation runs roughly US$5–20/month on annual plans, more month-to-month. Tiered "Plus" upsells (Speak) and long minimum commitments (Praktika, no monthly) are criticised. Human tutoring (Cambly, Cakap) is priced per session.

### Cited Findings
- ELSA (official): Free (ads, all features "Limited", no certificate courses); Premium 1 year US$159.99 (US$13.33/mo), 3 months US$59.99 (US$20/mo); 7-day free trial; lifetime "ELSA PRO pack" at $249.99 / $399.99; voice role-play limited on free, unlimited on Premium — [ELSA product page](https://elsanow.io/en/product). Third-party price claims conflict widely (e.g., $6.99–$89.99 Pro) — [Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/elsa-speak); [SoftwareFinder](https://softwarefinder.com/lms/elsa-speak/pricing). Regional pricing exists (e.g., Brazil BRL 37/mo, BRL 289/yr) — [SpeakShark Brazil](https://speakshark.com/blog/elsa-speak-price-brazil-2026)
- Speak: two tiers, Premium and Premium Plus; only Plus has unlimited custom lessons and "Made for You"; tier differences unclear at signup — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/speak-app-review/). Third-party prices conflict (~$17.99/mo or ~$83.99–99.99/yr) — [search summary incl. Toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/speak)
- Praktika: short free trial, then 3-month or annual plan only (no monthly); pricing varies by country; single tier — [LanguaTalk](https://languatalk.com/blog/praktika-review/)
- Gliglish: free 10 min/day, max 50 messages per conversation, limited availability at peak hours; Gliglish Plus unlimited — [Gliglish Help](https://gliglish.com/help); third-party price ~$29/mo or ~€23/mo annual (unverified) — [Genai.works](https://genai.works/applications/gliglish)
- Talkpal: free ~10 min/day; Premium annual reported $4.99/mo ($59.88/yr), others cite ~$13/mo; 14-day trial — [LanguaTalk TalkPal review](https://languatalk.com/blog/talkpal-review/); [Tooldirectory](https://tooldirectory.ai/tools/talkpal)
- Loora: free trial; third parties cite ~$15–20/month — [Loora](https://loora.com); [Aichief](https://aichief.com/ai-tools/loora/); [Practice Me](https://practiceme.app/vs/loora)
- Duolingo Video Call: part of Duolingo Max (highest tier) — [Duolingo blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/video-call)
- Cambly: no free tier; ~$8–17 per 30-min session depending on plan (competitor estimate) — [TalkDrill Cambly vs italki](https://www.talkdrill.com/blog/compare/cambly-vs-italki/)
- Cakap: from IDR 67,800 per session for English adult courses — [Cakap](https://cakap.com/en/cakap-package-price/)

### Inferences
- For Indonesia, ELSA's US$13.33/mo annual (~Rp 200k+/mo) is high relative to local purchasing power; a sub-Rp 50–100k/month tier, daily free minutes (like Gliglish/Talkpal) and local payment methods (QRIS, e-wallets) would be competitive. (Exchange-rate and willingness-to-pay figures not researched here.)
- Daily free minute caps are cheap to implement and match the "habit" loop; LLM + TTS + ASR cost per minute is the key unit-economics constraint (not researched here).

### Gaps
- Official Speak, Praktika, Loora, Talkpal Indonesia-region prices not verified (app store IDR prices not fetched).
- No data on conversion rates or retention for these freemium models.
